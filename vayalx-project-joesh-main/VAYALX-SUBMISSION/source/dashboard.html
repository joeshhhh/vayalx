🌾 Samba Paddy: Nursery to Harvest (A-Z)

Recommended varieties for Delta: ADT-53, CR-1009, CO-52. Seed rate: 12 kg/acre for SRI planting. Soak seeds in water with salt float test to eliminate chaffy seeds.

  • Nursery preparation with well-rotted FYM
  • Single seedling transplanting at 25x25 cm spacing
  • Cono-weeder operation at 10, 20, 30 days
  • Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) water management

🍌 Robusta / Grand Naine Banana High-Yield Guide

Pit size 60x60x60 cm. Spacing: 1.8 x 1.8 m (1,200 suckers/acre). Paring and pralinage sucker treatment with carbofuran & cow dung slurry prevents nematodes.

  • Desuckering every 45 days until bunch emergence
  • Denavelling (removal of male flower bud) for bunch weight
  • Bunch cover with perforated blue polythene sleeves
  • Prop with bamboo poles during heavy wind season
